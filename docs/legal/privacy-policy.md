## Readers
- We do not ask readers to create an account, and reading the site does not require giving us any personal information.
- We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
- Our hosting and content delivery providers ([Railway, Cloudflare]) process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect the site. They act on our behalf.
- We keep a log of visits by automated AI and search crawlers (the crawler's name, the page and the time). This log holds no information about human visitors.
- Search: when you search the site we store the search text and how many results it returned, to improve coverage. We do not store who searched.

## Organisers who submit an event
When you submit an event we ask for your name and a contact (email or phone number).
- **Why:** to check the submission with you and to tell you if we cannot publish it.
- **Who sees it:** only the people who run Pick the Joint. It is never shown on the site and never sold or shared for marketing.
- **How long:** kept while the listing is live and for [12 months] afterwards, then deleted.
- **Poster photos:** an image you upload is stored privately so we can read the details from it. It is never shown on the site and is deleted after the event has taken place.
- A spam check (Cloudflare Turnstile) runs on the form. Cloudflare processes technical data for that purpose under its own privacy policy.

## Information about venues, artists and organisers
Event, venue, artist and organiser pages are built from public announcements and other public sources, each linked on the page. Pages about people list professional, public facts only. If a page is about you and you want it corrected or removed, see /corrections.

## Automated tools
We use automated tools, including AI models provided by [Anthropic], to read public event announcements and write listings. Submissions may be read by these tools. We do not use your contact details to train any model.
